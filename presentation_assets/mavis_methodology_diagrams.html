Methodological Grounding: The first 10 readings compute the initial arithmetic baseline mean. The system assumes this calibration period represents a reasonable operational baseline, rather than guaranteeing resting conditions. Crucially, standard-deviation floors (σ_min) are individualized by physiological modality (0.35°C, 5.0 BPM, 2.5 br/min, 2.0%). Both EMA baseline adaptation and Welford variance updates are strictly skipped when any screening threshold is breached, preventing acute anomalies from polluting future reference profiles.

Multipliers represent heuristic risk amplifications, not diagnostic probabilities.
